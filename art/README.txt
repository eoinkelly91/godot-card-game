Card art goes in art/cards/<card id>.jpg (or .png), for example art/cards/gluto.jpg.
The card id is the "id" field in data/cards/*.json. A card with no file shows a coloured placeholder.
Images are shown in a wide box (about 3:1), so wide pictures work best; they are scaled to cover the box.
Placeholder art for champions came from the Cards.zip you uploaded (elite-o.jpg and lava_monster.jpg were
renamed to elite_o.jpg and lava_titan.jpg; blackthorn_dragon2.jpg was not used; Big Troll has no image yet).
